Privacy Policy for Notice

Last updated: 14 Aug 2026

Notice ("the app", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

What We Collect

Notice does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely on your device.

  • App tracking automations are set up and run through Apple's own Shortcuts app, on your device. Notice does not see, log, or store which apps you choose to track.
  • Timers, notifications, and alerts (via Apple's AlarmKit) run locally on your device.
  • Widgets and Dynamic Island displays run locally on your device.
  • Notice does not require an account, login, or any personal information to function.
  • Notice does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Crash Reports

If the app crashes, Apple's built-in diagnostic tools may collect anonymous crash logs to help us fix bugs. This is handled entirely by Apple's operating system-level reporting, not by Notice directly. You can review or turn this off in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children's Privacy

Notice does not knowingly collect any data from children, as it does not collect data from any user. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If Notice introduces new features that involve data collection (for example, optional future account-based features), this policy will be updated and the in-app App Privacy details will be revised accordingly.
